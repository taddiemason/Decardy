About Us

For more than a century, DeCardy Diecasting has been providing the highest quality zinc die cast parts to customers throughout the United States and around the world.

Specializing in high-precision and highly engineered zinc castings, DeCardy has helped companies like American Flyer, Snap-On, Harley Davidson and many others, solve their most difficult casting challenges.

Located in Chicago, Illinois, we combine decades of expertise with modern manufacturing capabilities to deliver exceptional results for our clients.

Our Services

High Pressure Zinc Die Casting

Precision zinc die casting services for high-quality, highly engineered parts. From miniature components to large castings using advanced Techmire and Frech equipment.

Secondary Services

Complete finishing and assembly services including machining, plating, painting, and assembly operations to deliver production-ready parts.

Design Assistance & Concurrent Engineering

Expert engineering support from concept to production, including design optimization, prototyping, and tooling consultation to ensure manufacturability and cost-effectiveness.
